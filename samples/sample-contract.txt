Service Agreement

This agreement is made between Acme Analytics and Northstar Retail for AI document processing services. The service provider shall process invoices, policies, and monthly reports for automated review.

The client must submit source documents by the fifth day of every month. Payment terms require the client to pay the total amount within 30 days of invoice receipt.

The agreement includes confidentiality obligations for all business records and customer data. Both parties should review data privacy controls before production deployment.

The contract is effective from 1 June 2026 and renews annually unless either party provides written termination notice.
